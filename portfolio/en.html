About me.

I’m a software developer with a Bachelor of Engineering in Health Technology. I’m currently studying for a master’s degree in Computer Technology at Aarhus University. What I enjoy most about development is following a problem from the moment someone describes what they need to the point where I can test a working solution.

For my bachelor’s project, TolkCare, I helped build an Android app for interpreting in healthcare. I worked on the steps that connect an appointment: booking an interpreter, sending a notification and starting a video call. I used Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and Firebase and tested how those features worked together.

From intern to student developer

CHROMAVISO A/S · SEP 2023 – OCT 2024

At Chromaviso, I developed and tested lighting control software for hospitals and care homes. I wrote unit tests, debugged existing features and helped deploy changes. That experience taught me how to improve a system that people already rely on every day.

What I’m working on now

My main interests during my master’s are software reliability and machine learning. In a team project, we’re investigating how well a model detects pedestrians at night and how its performance changes when we train it on images taken in different lighting conditions.
